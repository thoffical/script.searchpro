# Contributing to SearchPro

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the SearchPro add-on! We welcome contributions from the community to help improve the project. Please follow the guidelines below to ensure a smooth collaboration.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository**: Start by forking the repository to your own GitHub account.

2. **Create a Branch**: Create a new branch for your feature or bug fix. Use a descriptive name for your branch, such as `feature/add-search-filter` or `bugfix/fix-crash`.

3. **Make Your Changes**: Implement your changes in your branch. Be sure to write clear and concise commit messages.

4. **Test Your Changes**: Before submitting your pull request, test your changes to ensure everything works as expected.

5. **Submit a Pull Request**: Once you are satisfied with your changes, submit a pull request to the main repository. Provide a clear description of the changes you made and why they are necessary.

### Key Points to Remember:
- **Attribution**: Always give appropriate credit to the original creator when using or sharing the add-on.
- **NonCommercial Use**: The add-on is intended for non-commercial purposes only. Please do not use it for profit without permission.
- **ShareAlike**: If you modify or build upon this work, please share your contributions under the same license, ensuring that others can benefit from your improvements.

By following these guidelines, you help foster a community of collaboration and support for developers. Thank you for your understanding and for supporting open-source projects!

## Questions?

If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to open an issue in the repository or reach out directly.
